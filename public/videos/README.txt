Place your 4 video edit files in this directory:
- /public/videos/video1.mp4
- /public/videos/video2.mp4
- /public/videos/video3.mp4
- /public/videos/video4.mp4

Supported formats: MP4, WebM, MOV.
The application is configured to fetch these videos directly from /videos/video1.mp4, /videos/video2.mp4, /videos/video3.mp4, and /videos/video4.mp4.
If a local video file is not present yet, a romantic fallback video stream will automatically play in its place so the gallery is always functional!
